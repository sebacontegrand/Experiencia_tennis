# Product

## Register

brand

(The public site is the primary surface. The admin panel under `/admin` is product register: design serves Gabriela's daily work on her phone.)

## Users

- **Players:** adult amateur tennis players aged 35 to 60, mostly women, who play at a club or in a gated community. They travel for tennis and pay for something special. They arrive on their phone from an Instagram post or a WhatsApp message, often between other things, and decide quickly whether this is for them. On the page their job is to understand what the experience is (dates, place, what's included, price), then trust it and sign up or ask a question on WhatsApp.
- **Clubs, gated communities and hotels (B2B):** organizers who want to bring an experience to their members or guests. They need to see that it's serious and well organized, then get in touch.
- **Gabriela Bruno (admin):** the organizer and sports director. She is not technical and runs everything from her phone: prices, sign-ups, payments, the attendee list.

## Product Purpose

Experiencia Tenis organizes tennis experiences (Classic, Gold and Black) that combine play, travel, food and a group of people. The site exists to grow the player community, generate contacts with clubs and hotels, and take sign-ups for specific experiences with controlled capacity. Success looks like this: someone arriving from Instagram understands the experience in seconds, feels it was put together for them, and signs up or writes on WhatsApp without friction.

## Brand Personality

Exclusive, warm, careful. Gabriela Bruno's personal brand: a curated trip among friends, not a package. The voice is Rioplatense Spanish with voseo ("Jugá", "Sumate"), close and attentive, never corporate. The emotional goal: "this is a special trip, carefully put together, and I want to be in it." Premium the way a good boutique hotel is premium: attention to detail, calm, generous space, never ostentation.

## Anti-references

- **Over-the-top luxury:** gold everywhere, serif on everything, black-and-gold cliché, an attitude that feels pretentious or distant. Gold is a detail (thin lines, a highlight), never a surface.
- **Generic tennis-club site:** green and yellow, stock photos of balls and rackets, dated layouts, a results-and-fixtures feel.
- Also avoid the faceless travel-agency look (package grids, "book now" banners) and SaaS scaffolding (icon card grids, hero metrics, gradients).

## Design Principles

1. **Trust before sign-up.** Every page answers the real questions in order: what, when, where, what's included, how much, how to sign up. Missing data says "Consultar"; it is never invented.
2. **Curated, not loud.** Premium comes from restraint: space, rhythm, precise type, one accent. If a detail calls attention to itself, it goes.
3. **Close at hand.** WhatsApp and a person (Gabriela) are always one tap away. The tone is a host's, not a company's.
4. **Phone first, thumb first.** Most visits come from Instagram or WhatsApp on a phone: large targets, short scans, no horizontal scroll, the main action within reach.
5. **Honest imagery.** No photos that look like real participants and no invented testimonials. Until there are real photos, licensed stock stands in where people aren't the focus (courts, landscapes, players far away or from behind, details), captioned as illustrative where it could be read as ours.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 (gold is never small text on white; use `gold-deep`). Visible focus everywhere, labels on every field, 44px touch targets. Body text sized for readers 35–60 (16px minimum on mobile, generous line height). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
